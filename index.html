How to Play

Rules, steering controls, food varieties & scoring mechanics.

Desktop & Laptop Keyboard
WASD or Arrow Keys Turn snake smoothly in 4 directions
Space or P Instant pause or resume game
R Quick restart run
M Toggle sound effects on or off
T / O / H Open Achievements (T), Settings (O), or Guide (H)
Mobile & Tablet Controls
On-Screen D-Pad

Tap Up, Down, Left, Right chevrons. Center circle pauses.

Swipe Anywhere on Board

Flick your finger across the playfield to steer seamlessly.

Classic Apple +10 Pts × Combo

Always on the board. Each apple increases your snake's length by 1 segment and charges your combo streak.

Golden Star Fruit +35 Pts • +2.0s Combo

Spawns periodically for 8 seconds. Awards massive points and instantly extends your active combo window!

Chill Berry +20 Pts • Pace Slowed 6s

Rare ice blue fruit. Reduces frantic movement speed by 25% for 6 seconds, granting easy maneuvering in tight spaces.

Combo Streak Multiplier

Eating another food item before your combo bar drains levels up your multiplier from x1 up to x5! At x5, every food item awards quintuple points, propelling your score to record levels.

Game Modes & Speeds
  • Relax (160ms): Generous 5.5s combo timer. Perfect for casual, peaceful play.
  • Normal (115ms): Balanced 4.5s combo timer. Classic authentic arcade experience.
  • Fast (80ms): 3.8s combo timer with higher base points (+20% bonus) for reflex masters.
  • Dynamic: Begins at comfortable 135ms and accelerates down to 60ms as your snake grows.
Boundaries (Wall Modes)

• Solid Walls: Crashing into the border ends the run.
• Portal Wrap: Slithering into an edge warps your snake through to the opposite side!
